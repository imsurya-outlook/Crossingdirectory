Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Our Shops provides a local business directory for Crossing Republic. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the website.

Information we collect

  • Account details, including your name and email address, when you register or sign in.
  • Business listing details, including names, descriptions, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and images.
  • Ratings and reviews submitted while signed in.
  • Anonymous visitor information used to prevent duplicate view and detail-click analytics.

How we use information

We use account information to authenticate users and manage permissions. Listing and contact information is used to publish business advertisements. Ratings and anonymous usage counts help operate and improve the directory.

Cookies and sign-in

We use essential cookies for signed-in sessions and a durable anonymous visitor cookie (cd_visitor) to distinguish anonymous activity. The visitor cookie does not contain account information. If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides account details needed to create or connect your account, subject to Google's privacy practices.

Public information

Information you add to an advertisement may be visible to visitors, including business addresses and public contact details. Do not publish personal or confidential information that you do not want to be public.

Third-party resources

The website loads Bootstrap and Bootstrap Icons styles from jsDelivr. These providers may receive standard request information, such as your IP address, when your browser requests those resources.

Data access and deletion

Authorized account holders and administrators may manage listings and account information. To request correction or deletion of account or listing information, contact the site administrator through the channel provided by the website operator.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website changes. The latest update date will be shown at the top of this page.
